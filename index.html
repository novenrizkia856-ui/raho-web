How it works

Detect. Migrate. Verify.

Every account moves through the same visible stages. Nothing is marked active until Raho has checked it.

  1. 1
    Account
    account.detect()
    Reads the scheme that signs today, like ECDSA
  2. 2
    Key
    pqKey.prepare()
    Prepares or registers the post quantum key
    key.register()
    scheme.select()
  3. 3
    Raho
    migration.build()
    Checks compatibility, then builds the migration action for review
    compatible
    reviewed
    executed
  4. 4
    Verify
    auth.verify()
    Reads the account again and confirms post quantum authentication is active.
