Why agents need a firewall

Two ways to hand an agent a wallet. Both break.

Route AGive it the keys

One bad output is final

A confused or manipulated agent can move anything. Onchain, there is no undo.

No limits at all

Every asset, every contract, every amount. Forever.

No record of intent

You see what happened. Never what it tried.

Route BSign everything yourself

The agent waits on you

Every routine payment needs your click. Autonomy disappears.

Fatigue wins

After the hundredth prompt, people approve without reading.

Still no rules

A tired click is not a policy. Nothing stops the next mistake.

One fix for both sides:
bounded authority, checked on every request.

    Pause Agent

    Revoke Session

    Ends its current authority now. A new session is needed to act.
